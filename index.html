Pratyush Potu

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. student in the Division of Applied Mathematics at Brown University, advised by Johnny Guzmán.

I am broadly interested in numerical methods for partial differential equations. During my Ph.D., I have explored how ideas from topology, geometry, and combinatorics can be used in the development and analysis of these methods.

Before Brown, I received a B.Sc. in Mathematics from the University of Texas at Austin, with a certificate in Scientific Computation and Data Science. I am grateful to be currently supported by the Center for Information Geometric Mechanics and Optimization (CIGMO).

Research

My research concerns structure-preserving discretization of partial differential equations (PDE). The philosophy behind this research is that numerical methods should preserve key properties satisfied by solutions of the PDE being approximated. Methods built in this way are generally stable and avoid spurious, unphysical solutions.
